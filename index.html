Lu Li 李璐

I'm a second-year PhD student at Mila – Quebec AI Institute and Université de Montréal, advised by Pierre-Luc Bacon. My research centers on reinforcement learning and deep learning. Before Mila, I earned an M.S. in Electronic and Information Engineering from Tsinghua University, and a B.S. in Computer Science from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.
